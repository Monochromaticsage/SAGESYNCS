Straight answers

The questions agents ask before a first call.

Are the names and numbers in the walkthrough real?

No. They’re examples to show how it works. Your results depend on how many leads you get today and how fast you reply now. We look at that together on the call.

What if it says something wrong to a lead?

It only answers from listing details and FAQs you approve. It never promises prices or terms, and anything it isn’t sure about goes to you. For the first two weeks I read every conversation.

What about fair housing?

It never describes or rates neighborhoods, safety or schools, and never steers by protected characteristics. It points leads to public sources or to you instead.

Is it compliant with texting rules?

It only replies to people who just contacted you, says who it’s from, stops immediately on STOP and only texts during reasonable hours. I’m not a lawyer, so I recommend your broker confirms your lead forms include texting consent.

Will leads know it’s AI?

If they ask, it says honestly that it’s your assistant and offers to get you on the phone.

Do I have to change my CRM?

No. Follow Up Boss is the simplest to connect. For other CRMs I work from your lead notification emails. You keep getting leads the way you do now.
